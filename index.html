SN1 Reaction

Definition & Mechanism

SN1 (Substitution Nucleophilic Unimolecular) reactions proceed through a carbocation intermediate.

  • Multi-step reaction mechanism
  • Rate depends only on the substrate concentration
  • Carbocation rearrangements are possible

Rate Law

rate = k [R–X]

Stereochemistry

The planar carbocation allows nucleophilic attack from both sides, producing a racemic mixture.

SN2 Reaction

Definition & Mechanism

SN2 (Substitution Nucleophilic Bimolecular) reactions occur in one concerted step through a backside attack.

  • Single-step (concerted) mechanism
  • No intermediate species
  • Strongly affected by steric hindrance

Rate Law

rate = k [R–X][Nu⁻]

Stereochemistry

Backside attack leads to Walden inversion, resulting in 100% inversion of configuration.

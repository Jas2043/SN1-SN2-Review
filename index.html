SN1 Reaction

Definition & Mechanism

SN1 (Substitution Nucleophilic Unimolecular) proceeds through a carbocation intermediate.

  • Multi-step reaction
  • Rate depends only on substrate
  • Carbocation rearrangements possible

Rate Law

rate = k [R–X]

Stereochemistry

Planar carbocation leads to racemization (attack from both sides).

SN2 Reaction

Definition & Mechanism

SN2 (Substitution Nucleophilic Bimolecular) occurs in one concerted step.

  • Backside attack
  • No intermediate
  • Sensitive to steric hindrance

Rate Law

rate = k [R–X][Nu⁻]

Stereochemistry

Backside attack causes Walden inversion (100% inversion).
